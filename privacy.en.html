Data stored on your device

The app stores language, sound and appearance preferences, and game progress or statistics where implemented. A selected avatar image is resized and stored locally. The app does not provide for uploading that photo to a developer-operated server.

The app includes the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK; the current placements are demonstration IDs. The SDK may process technical data, advertising identifiers, and ad interaction information. Exact processing details and retention must be checked against the release build and current Yandex documents.

Yandex privacy policy: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/

Bluetooth

Bluetooth multiplayer exchanges connection information and game events necessary for a match. Technical identifier handling must be verified on actual devices.

Deletion and requests

To remove locally stored data, use Android Settings → Apps → STRICT RAMS → Storage → Clear data. This also removes your locally saved avatar. Advertising partners have their own deletion processes.
